[TITLE] Hiểu quan hệ nguyên nhân và đối lập
[CONTENT]
Hướng dẫn: because chỉ nguyên nhân; so/therefore chỉ kết quả; although/however chỉ đối lập. Đọc cả hai vế trước khi chọn.

A DIFFERENT WAY TO START THE DAY
Westfield School moved its first lesson from 7:30 to 8:15 after students reported feeling tired in early classes. Because many students travelled a long way, the old timetable required them to wake up before six. The later start gives them more time to sleep. Teachers have noticed fewer late arrivals, although they have not yet measured changes in exam scores.
The change also created a problem. Lessons now finish forty-five minutes later, so some students miss the bus they previously used. The school therefore arranged a new afternoon bus service. This solved the transport problem for most students, but the service costs money. To keep the cost manageable, the school shares the bus with a nearby college.
Some parents initially opposed the new timetable because it affected family routines. However, a survey after six weeks found that most parents now preferred it. The head teacher says the school will keep the new schedule for one term and review it before making a permanent decision. The school does not claim that later starts improve every aspect of learning; its current evidence concerns attendance and tiredness.
