Daniel: Welcome to Brook Community Centre. There is no charge to become a member, although individual activities may have their own small fees. You do not need to visit every day. Choose activities that fit your schedule. If you want to attend an exercise class, put your name on the class list before the day of the lesson. Some classes are popular and places are limited. When a class is full, we record your name and contact you if a place becomes available. You can borrow sports equipment while you are here. Please hand it back before you go home; equipment cannot be taken away overnight. If you are not sure which activity would suit you, speak to our reception team. They can explain what each class involves and help you find a suitable starting point. We hope you enjoy your first visit.
