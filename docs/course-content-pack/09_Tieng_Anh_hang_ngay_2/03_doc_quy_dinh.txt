RIVER LIBRARY — VISITOR RULES
The library opens at 9 a.m. and closes at 6 p.m. Please speak quietly. You may use your phone to read or send messages, but phone calls must be made outside. Food is not allowed. You can bring water in a bottle with a lid. Visitors may borrow up to three books at a time. Books must be returned within fourteen days. To borrow books, show your library card at the front desk. If you do not have a card, ask a member of staff for a registration form. Children under ten must stay with an adult. Computers are available on the first floor. Please book a computer before using it.
