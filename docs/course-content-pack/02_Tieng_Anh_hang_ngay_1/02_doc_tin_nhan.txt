[TITLE] Đọc tin nhắn hẹn gặp
[CONTENT]
Hướng dẫn: Đọc toàn bộ tin nhắn để tìm lịch cuối cùng, không dừng ở đề xuất đầu. instead = thay vào đó; entrance = lối vào.

Linh, 9:00 a.m.: Hi Sam. Would you like to meet at the City Library on Friday? We can study English at 4 p.m.
Sam, 9:10 a.m.: I finish work at 4:30. Can we meet at 5 p.m. instead?
Linh, 9:15 a.m.: Yes, 5 p.m. is fine. The library closes at six, so we have one hour. Please bring your English notebook.
Sam, 9:20 a.m.: Great. Should I bring some snacks?
Linh, 9:25 a.m.: No, food is not allowed inside. Let's meet at the main entrance, beside the big clock.
Sam, 9:30 a.m.: OK. I will take the bus. See you on Friday.
