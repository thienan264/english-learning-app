SUN CAFE
We open from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday to Saturday. We are closed on Sunday.
Breakfast is served until 11 a.m.: egg sandwich — $4; fruit bowl — $3.
Lunch is served from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m.: chicken rice — $7; vegetable noodles — $6.
Drinks are available all day: tea — $2; coffee — $3; orange juice — $4. Tea can be hot or cold.
The vegetable noodles contain no meat. If you cannot eat eggs, please tell us before ordering. You can eat inside or take your food away. Card payment is accepted for orders of $5 or more. Free drinking water is available near the counter.
