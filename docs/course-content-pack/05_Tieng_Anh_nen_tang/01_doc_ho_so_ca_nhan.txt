[TITLE] Đọc hồ sơ cá nhân
[CONTENT]
Hướng dẫn: Đọc câu hỏi rồi tìm câu có thông tin tương ứng. Phân biệt nơi xuất thân với nơi đang sống. nurse = y tá; shift = ca làm việc. Ví dụ: “Anna is 24” trả lời “How old is Anna?”.

My name is Anna. I am 24 years old and I come from Da Nang. I now live in Hanoi because I work at a children's hospital there. I am a nurse. I share a small apartment with my sister, Mai. She is a university student. From Monday to Friday, my shift starts at seven in the morning. I usually take the bus to work. After work, I cook dinner and read books. On Saturdays, I visit a nearby park with Mai. We enjoy walking there. I am learning English because I want to talk to visitors from other countries at the hospital.
