GREEN COMMUNITY CENTRE — ENGLISH CLUB
The English Club meets every Tuesday from 6:00 to 7:00 p.m. in Room 3. The first meeting this month is on 6 May. The club is for adults who want to practise simple English. Students can join too, but children under twelve cannot attend. Please bring a notebook and a pen. You do not need to bring a textbook. Joining the club is free. Water is available in the room. To join, send your name to Ms Hoa by email before Monday evening. The centre opens at 5:30 p.m., so you can arrive early. The Saturday art class takes place in Room 2 and is a different activity.
