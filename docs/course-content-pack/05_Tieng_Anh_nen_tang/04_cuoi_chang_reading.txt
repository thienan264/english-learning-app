MEET PAUL
Paul is twenty-eight. He is a cook at a small hotel in Bristol. He lives with his cousin, Jack, who works in a bookshop. Paul starts work at two in the afternoon and finishes at ten at night. He goes to work by bicycle. On Mondays, his day off, he enjoys drawing at home.

HOTEL NOTICE
The hotel's beginner cooking class takes place on Sunday, from 10 a.m. to noon, in the ground-floor kitchen. Adults and teenagers aged sixteen or over can attend. Bring a small container to take your food home. Ingredients and cooking tools are provided. The class costs twelve pounds. Please book by Friday at the hotel reception. There is no class on Saturday.
